# Medical Wellness Academy Website Plan

## Positioning
Premium professional training academy in London, built around safe practice, clinical judgement, professional scope and practical competency where applicable.

## Design system
- Original MWA logo, unchanged.
- Cream / warm ivory primary background.
- Gold primary accent, including the deeper rich-gold direction used in current MWA training materials.
- Dark charcoal reserved mainly for typography and contrast.
- Genuine 61 Mansell Street clinic imagery.
- Editorial serif headings with clean sans-serif body copy.
- No generic AI clinician or learner stock.

## Conversion path
Course discovery → course detail → enquiry → eligibility check → confirmed dates / fees / status → booking.

The website does not use an aggressive instant-purchase flow before eligibility is understood.

## Accuracy controls
- No MWA accreditation claim unless course-specific status is formally confirmed.
- No CPDSO trustmark while the legacy Nova provider-name transfer remains unconfirmed.
- Anatomy & Physiology is not described as a regulated qualification without the appropriate awarding-body arrangement.
- Attendance is not described as automatic certification.
- Course completion is not described as automatic authority to practise.
- Product-specific protocols are not generalised across Exosomes, Polynucleotides, Collagen Biostimulators, IM Vitamin Injections, Fat Dissolving or similar treatment categories.
- Prices and dates are held back until final approved commercial details are supplied.
