FAQs

Questions before you enrol.

Clear answers on eligibility, delivery, assessment, certification, cancellations and course status.

Who are the courses for?

The Academy reviews your qualifications and relevant experience against each programme. Exact entry requirements, including the position for applicants still studying, must be confirmed before enrolment.

How many courses are in the current Academy library?

The internal submission pack covers 18 proposed activities. Pathway to Aesthetics and the Wellness Injection Masterclass are combined programmes with their own timetables and award requirements; the activity count is not a certificate promise.

How long are the individual courses?

Pathway to Aesthetics is three days, on 28, 29 and 30 October 2026, at £1,999. The Wellness Injection Masterclass is one day at £599; its date is to be confirmed. Individual activity durations and combined-programme timetables are confirmed separately.

Where does training take place?

61 Mansell Street, London E1 8AN.

Does attendance guarantee certification?

No. Learners must meet the published attendance, knowledge and practical competency requirements for the relevant programme.

Is practical training included?

Where a course includes a practical treatment or clinical skill, supervised practical competency may form part of the programme and assessment.

Does a certificate mean I am automatically allowed to practise?

No. Learners remain responsible for professional scope, competence, professional registration where applicable, insurance and legal or regulatory requirements.

Is Anatomy & Physiology a regulated qualification?

Anatomy and physiology content within these programmes is not confirmation of a formal Level 3 qualification.

Are the courses accredited?

The Academy only describes a course as accredited where that status applies to the specific course at the relevant time. Course-specific status is confirmed before booking.

What are the cancellation terms?

The student agreement and cancellation, refund and transfer policy must be agreed for the programme before booking. Existing draft thresholds must not be treated as approved terms for these combined offers.

What happens if the Academy cancels a course?

The learner is offered a transfer or a full refund of the course fee paid for the cancelled course.

How do I find dates and fees?

Send an enquiry with the programme you are interested in. Final dates, fees, prerequisites and course-specific certification or accreditation status are confirmed before booking.

Still unsure?

Ask before you book.

Tell us your professional background and the course you are considering. We will confirm whether it is an appropriate route.
