The training environment

Learn in a clinical setting, not a generic classroom.

Medical Wellness Academy is based at 61 Mansell Street, London E1 8AN. Training is structured around the decisions that sit behind treatment: assessment, consultation, contraindications, consent, documentation, complications awareness and aftercare.
