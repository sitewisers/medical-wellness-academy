The Academy

A clinical setting built for professional learning.

Medical Wellness Academy is based at 61 Mansell Street, London E1 8AN. Our training brings professional education into a clinical environment, with programmes structured around safe decision-making, treatment planning and practical development where appropriate.

Course design centres on anatomy, safe consultation, contraindications, infection control, complications awareness, professional scope and practical competency where relevant.

About the Academy
How we train

Professional judgement, not just technique.

Training is built to help learners understand when, why and whether a treatment or clinical skill should be performed, not just the mechanics.

Eligibility before enrolment

Course-specific entry requirements, professional scope and insurance considerations are confirmed before booking.

Knowledge plus practical assessment

Assessment reflects the course. Practical competency is assessed where the programme includes a practical skill.

Clear limits on certification

Course completion is not presented as automatic authority to practise.

Before you enrol

Know exactly what you are booking.

Who are the courses for?

Entry requirements depend on the programme and practical activities. Tell the Academy your qualifications and experience so your eligibility can be reviewed before booking.

Does attendance guarantee certification?

No. Learners must meet the requirements of the relevant programme.

Are the courses accredited?

The Academy only describes a course as accredited when that status applies to that specific programme at that time. Course-specific status is confirmed before booking.
