# Medical Wellness Academy website

## Structure
- Home
- Courses index
- Pathway to Aesthetics flagship page
- 12 individual one-day course pages
- The Academy
- Academy Training
- FAQs
- Enquiry
- Privacy Notice
- Website Terms & Cookie Notice
- Cancellation, Refund & Transfer Policy

## Design rules applied
- Original transparent MWA logo retained unchanged
- Cream and gold are the primary visual system
- Dark colour is used only for text/contrast, not as a dominant brand background
- Genuine 61 Mansell Street clinic imagery is used
- Separate pages rather than one long landing page
- No unverified accreditation, qualification, trainer, pricing or insurance claims

## Before deployment
- Confirm final Academy domain
- Confirm production enquiry email/form backend
- Add approved dates and fees when final
- Confirm any course-specific accreditation/CPD status before displaying it publicly
- Connect analytics only after cookie-consent implementation if non-essential tracking is used
