Academy Training

Clear expectations from enrolment to assessment.

Professional training is built around eligibility, safe practice, assessment and the learner's own professional responsibilities.

01

Eligibility before enrolment

The Academy reviews your qualifications and experience against the entry requirements for the programme and its practical activities. Applicants who are still studying must also have their eligibility confirmed before enrolment.

02

Face-to-face learning

The current course library is built around professional face-to-face training at 61 Mansell Street, London E1 8AN. Pathway to Aesthetics takes place on 28, 29 and 30 October 2026 at £1,999. The separate Wellness Injection Masterclass is one day at £599, with its date to be confirmed. Combined timetables, preparation and practical arrangements are confirmed before enrolment.

03

Knowledge & practical assessment

Assessment varies by programme. Knowledge assessment and trainer-observed practical competency are used where appropriate to the course.

04

Certification with limits

Attendance alone does not guarantee certification. A certificate does not replace professional registration, insurance, legal requirements, clinical governance or the learner's responsibility to work within competence.
