Learning content

Understand the treatment. Develop your judgement.

Explore the assessment, preparation and safety principles behind four clinical skill areas, alongside consent, documentation and aftercare.

Phlebotomy

Blood collection techniques, patient identification and safe sample handling.

IV therapy

IV cannulation and infusion principles, preparation, monitoring and recognising when to escalate concerns.

IM vitamin injections

Vitamin selection principles, suitability, contraindications and IM injection techniques within an approved clinical framework.

PRP

PRP preparation, centrifugation and treatment principles, linked to the selected system and approved protocol.

Patient assessment, treatment suitability and contraindications; infection prevention; recognising complications and responding appropriately; consultation, consent, documentation and aftercare.
