Course focus

Microneedling

Skin anatomy and physiology, consultation, contraindications, microneedling theory, equipment and product knowledge, aftercare and supervised practical competency where applicable.

Skin anatomy & physiology

Structure and function of the skin, wound healing and collagen-induction context.

Consultation & suitability

Medical history, expectations, informed consent and absolute or relative contraindications.

Equipment & product knowledge

Device, needle or cartridge considerations and appropriate product-use principles.

Aftercare & competency

Post-treatment advice, safe-practice review and supervised competency where applicable.

Assessment & certification

Competency matters.

Attendance alone does not guarantee certification. Learners must meet the published attendance, knowledge and practical competency requirements for the relevant course. Practical assessment is used where the programme includes a practical skill.

Professional responsibility

Course completion does not by itself authorise a learner to practise. Learners remain responsible for professional scope, competence, insurance and any applicable legal or regulatory requirements.
